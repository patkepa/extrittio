# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Extrittio a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and repair harm where possible.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository owner. Maintainers will protect
reporter confidentiality where possible, investigate fairly, and may issue a
warning, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant depending on impact and recurrence. A maintainer involved in a report
must recuse themselves from its handling.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing
the project. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
