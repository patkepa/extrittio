# Code of Conduct

This project is intended to be a practical, respectful place to collaborate.

Expected behavior:

- Be direct, constructive, and respectful.
- Keep technical criticism focused on the work.
- Assume public discussions may be read by future contributors.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that make collaboration harder or unsafe. Serious or repeated violations may lead to blocked participation in the repository.
